Put the Game Vault on your iPhone

Do this once, then open the games from the new icon on your Home Screen.

  1. Tap Share □↑It is at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Tap “Add to Home Screen”Scroll down in the Share menu if you do not see it.
  3. Tap “Add”The Game Vault icon will appear on your Home Screen.

Can’t find “Add to Home Screen”? Open this page in the Safari app first, then try the steps again.
